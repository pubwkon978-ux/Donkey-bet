2. Game Rules

Games use a standard 52-card deck. The player who sends the game request is the Dealer. The opponent Predicts the card. If the named card goes to the Predictor, the Predictor wins. If it goes to the Dealer, the Dealer wins. All decisions are final once dealing begins.
