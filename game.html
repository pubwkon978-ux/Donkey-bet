Games use a standard 52-card deck. The Dealer deals cards one at a time. The Predictor names a card before dealing begins. If that card is dealt to the Predictor's position, Predictor wins. If the deck is exhausted without the named card appearing, Dealer wins.

Top-ups and withdrawals are processed manually by the admin. Balances are non-transferable.
